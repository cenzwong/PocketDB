# Risk register

Probability and impact are initial qualitative estimates. All entries are open and unmeasured at plan creation. The responsible workstream reviews the trigger and records evidence or revised exposure at the listed gate.

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Trigger | Mitigation | Responsible workstream | Review status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Direct peers cannot connect | High | High | Cross-network ICE fails or pairing has repeated timeouts | Configurable STUN/TURN, observed path diagnostics and supported network documentation | Networking | Open · TASK-004, TASK-032 |
| Public discovery outage or dependency changes | Medium | High | Selected service is unavailable, rate-limited or changes terms | Replaceable discovery adapter, manual experiment baseline and service documentation | Networking | Open · TASK-004, TASK-026 |
| Host browser suspends execution | High | High | Background, sleep or close drops worker or session | Detect lifecycle changes, warn users, recover with bounded reconnection and explicit outcomes | Runtime and SDK | Open · TASK-010, TASK-038 |
| Native bridge compatibility is narrower than needed | High | High | Driver startup, bound parameters, transactions or commands fail | Probe early, publish verified subsets and decide a staged native rollout | Native bridges | Open · TASK-008, TASK-009, TASK-049 |
| PostgreSQL sessions interfere or read-only permissions fail | Medium | High | Cross-client transaction state leaks or an unauthorized write succeeds | Validate session isolation and use verified engine controls with fail-closed behavior | PostgreSQL and security | Open · TASK-006, TASK-029 |
| Browser storage evicts or cannot persist data | High | High | Quota exceeded, private browsing disables storage or saved state disappears | Prefer supported adapters, surface limits, verify reload and portable export/restore | Persistence | Open · TASK-005, TASK-033 |
